The week ahead
on the markets

Every Monday: the main context, the day-by-day calendar, prices and the one event worth watching.

Latest issue · November 23, 2026

November 23–27

🏦 Main context: the minutes of the Fed's October meeting showed that almost all policymakers expect further rate hikes, and markets are pricing in a high probability of a hike at the December 9 meeting. It is a short week: the US is closed on Thursday.

📅 The week: Mon–Tue — quieter than usual, no key US data; Wed — second estimate of US Q3 GDP, October PCE inflation, weekly jobless claims (moved from Thursday) and the Fed's Beige Book; Thu — Thanksgiving, US markets closed; Fri — US stocks close early (1:00 p.m. New York time), monthly BTC and ETH options expire on Deribit.

📊 Gold ~$3,626 · Bitcoin ~$61,400 · EUR/USD ~1.098 · GBP/USD ~1.310 — data as of November 23.

💡 The key event of the week is PCE inflation on Wednesday: the Fed's favourite gauge. Above expectations — hikes look more certain, the dollar firms, gold comes under pressure; below — the opposite.

November 16–20 · November 16, 2026

🏦 Main context: US inflation came in above expectations, markets are pricing in Fed rate hikes, and the dollar hit fresh annual highs. It is a heavy week for central bank speakers.

📅 The week: Mon — Canada CPI, Fed Chair Warsh speaks; Tue — RBA and RBNZ meeting minutes, UK employment, US retail sales; Wed — UK CPI, Bailey and Lagarde speak, Fed meeting minutes; Thu — Australian employment, US jobless claims and Philly Fed index; Fri — China LPR, UK retail sales, University of Michigan sentiment (US).

📊 Gold ~$3,847 · Bitcoin ~$64,600 · EUR/USD ~1.103 · GBP/USD ~1.322 — data as of November 16.

💡 The key event of the week is the Fed minutes on Wednesday: how seriously a rate hike was discussed. A strong dollar and rate-hike expectations usually weigh on gold; a softer tone does the opposite.

9–13 November · November 9, 2026

🏦 The Fed stays hawkish: the market puts the odds of a December rate hike at around 80 %. The Bank of Japan held at 1.25 %, the Bank of Canada hiked at its last meeting — both publish minutes, and the market is looking for hints on the next step.

📅 The week: Mon — FOMC members speak; Tue — US inflation (CPI), Bank of Japan and Bank of Canada minutes; Wed — bank holiday in the US and Canada, calendar is empty; Thu — UK GDP; Fri — US producer prices (PPI).

📊 Gold ~$4,109 · Bitcoin ~$74,230 · EUR/USD ~1.1150 · GBP/USD ~1.3362 — data as of 9 November.

💡 The main trigger this week is CPI on Tuesday: if inflation comes in softer than expected, December hike talk fades and gold with the euro get support; if it runs hotter, the dollar goes up and gold stays under pressure.

⚠️ Not investment advice.

2–6 November · November 2, 2026

🏦 The Fed kept rates at 3.75–4.00% on 28 October — but two members voted for a hike, so December is back in play. This week the RBA decides (Tuesday, a +25bp move is expected) and the Bank of England on Thursday.

📅 The week: Mon — US ISM Manufacturing; Tue — RBA, Swiss CPI, US midterm elections, JOLTS; Wed — New Zealand employment, euro-area flash CPI, ISM Services, crude inventories; Thu — Bank of England, US jobless claims; Fri — Non-Farm Payrolls and Canadian employment, Ivey PMI, Michigan sentiment.

📊 Gold ~$3,968 (Friday closed near $3,899) · Bitcoin ~$77.8k · EUR/USD ~1.116 · GBP/USD ~1.339 — data as of 02.11.

💡 The main trigger is Friday's US jobs report plus the midterm results mid-week. If the labour market looks weak, the December hike debate shifts again — for gold that is the main argument higher.

⚠️ Not investment advice.

🏦 The Fed hiked in September and holds on 28.10. Markets price a December hike: Warsh's tone matters more than the decision.

📅 This week: Mon — German Ifo expectations 91.6; Tue — US consumer confidence; Wed — Fed 🔴 and Bank of Canada; Thu — ECB, US GDP, PCE; Fri — eurozone GDP, BoJ.

📊 Gold ~$4,008 (above $4,000) · Bitcoin ~$80.1k · EUR/USD ~1.122 · GBP/USD ~1.337 — as of 26.10.

💡 Main trigger — the Fed on Wednesday: the hold is priced, so markets will watch the December hints.

19–23 October · October 19, 2026

🏦 US inflation came in at 3.7% y/y — above forecast, with PPI at +1.3% m/m. The Fed meets on 28.10: markets price a hike, not a cut.

📅 This week: Mon — Canada CPI; Tue — US housing data; Wed — UK CPI 🔴; Thu — New Zealand CPI; Fri — PMIs and retail sales.

📊 Gold ~$4,165 (near highs) · Bitcoin ~$82.3k · EUR/USD ~1.121 · GBP/USD ~1.334 — UK CPI on Wednesday decides.

💡 The main trigger is UK CPI on Wednesday: hot data pressures risk assets, gold holds up.

⚠️ Not investment advice.

12–16 October · October 12, 2026

🌍 News

🏦 US inflation week. September CPI lands on Wednesday: forecast +0.6% m/m and 3.6% y/y (previously 3.4%). Fed commentary points to the debate being about further tightening, not easing.

🛢 Middle East: reports of explosions in the Strait of Hormuz, yet oil is falling early in the week — the market is reading it as local risk, not a supply disruption.

📅 Calendar (Montenegro time)

▸ Tue 13.10 — Lagarde speaks (ECB)

▸ Wed 14.10, 14:30 — US CPI 🔴 (forecast 3.6% y/y)

▸ Thu 15.10, 08:00 — UK August GDP (forecast −0.1% m/m)

▸ Thu 15.10, 14:30 — PPI, retail sales, jobless claims, Philly Fed

📊 Markets

🥇 Gold: buyers stay in control, the market is testing a break of $4,200. A strong dollar caps the upside; CPI is the trigger.

₿ Bitcoin: recovered to ~$84,000 after a slide to $80,000. Support $77–80k, analyst targets range from $80k to $92k.

💶 EUR/USD: the dollar sits near an 18-month high, the euro is under pressure; DailyFX sees a double-bottom attempt.

💷 GBP/USD: the pound is under pressure ahead of the UK budget, plus a weak GDP forecast on Thursday.

💡 The week's main trigger is US CPI on Wednesday: hot data pushes the dollar up and pressures gold and crypto; soft data gives a bounce.
