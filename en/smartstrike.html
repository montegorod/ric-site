FAQ

What is SmartStrike?

Automated, real-time copying of our team's trading to your account. We never get access to your funds.

What is the minimum?

$1,000 per trading account. You fund your own broker account first — we charge nothing upfront.

How much does it cost?

No upfront fee. Our fee is 50% of profit, settled weekly.

Which broker should I choose?

Ryumount — open to all countries, including the US. VT Markets — everyone except US citizens.

Can I lose my deposit?

Every trade has a stop-loss set in advance, so the loss on it is limited. But a week or a month can still end in a loss — that is a normal part of trading.

How do I withdraw?

You request a withdrawal yourself in the broker's client area, any time after trades are closed. Withdraw profit or let it keep working.

Will I see the trades?

Yes. All open positions and history are visible in your MetaTrader terminal.

What leverage is used?

Depends on the broker — 1:500 or 1:1000. It does not affect the trading itself.

Do you teach trading or reveal the strategy?

No. We don't discuss the strategy or algorithms. For self-study there is our free trading guide.
