How risk works after a loss

After a losing trade the system does not reduce the trade size; it keeps trading the same size as before the loss. This gets the account out of a drawdown faster. Once the loss is recovered, the size is recalculated for the current deposit.

The flip side: while the account is in a drawdown, each next trade carries a higher risk relative to the deposit. So we monitor this manually: if losses come in a streak (for example, 3 stops in a row on the aggressive model), we switch this mode off and move the account to normal risk — a share of the current deposit. The deposit then lasts much longer, but recovery takes more time.

Which model to choose

No one can say in advance which model will perform best: it depends on the future market. If the market is active and losing streaks are rare, the aggressive model may earn more, even if recovery once takes a month. If drawdowns happen several times a year, the standard model may end up ahead of the aggressive one.

What matters most is how calmly you handle drawdowns. Ask yourself whether you are comfortable seeing minus 3%, 7% or 15% on a single trade, and choose accordingly. Not sure? Start with the demo.

How much does it cost?

No upfront fee. Our fee is 50% of profit, settled weekly.

Which broker should I choose?

Ryumount — open to all countries, including the US. VT Markets — everyone except US citizens.

Can there be losses?

Every trade has a stop-loss set in advance, so the loss on it is limited. But a week or a month can still end in a loss — that is a normal part of trading.

How do I withdraw?

You request a withdrawal yourself in the broker's client area, any time after trades are closed. Withdraw profit or let it keep working.

Yes. All open positions and history are visible in your MetaTrader terminal.

All trades are visible in your MetaTrader. For convenience you can connect your account to FXmonitor: balance, profit and drawdown on your phone, updated every 10 seconds. We use it to show our live account too.
